2

Android Connection Guide

USB-C & Portable Hotspot

Connect your Android device directly to this PC without any local Wi-Fi router

1
Connect Cable Connect your Android phone to PC using a USB cable.
2
Enable USB Tethering On Android: Open Settings → Network & Internet (or Connections) → Hotspot & Tethering and turn USB tethering ON.
3
Open Pairing Link On Android Chrome, visit https://airodrop.site/install. AiroDrop auto-connects to PC.
Pro Tip: If USB tethering is greyed out, make sure the USB mode popup on your phone is set to "File Transfer" or "Tethering".

Android Setup Guide

How to Install AiroDrop App on Android (Chrome / Browser)

STEP 1 Open in Chrome

Open Chrome or default Android browser & go to https://airodrop.site/install.

STEP 2 Tap Three Dots (⋮)

Tap the Three-Dot Menu icon (⋮) at top right of Chrome.

STEP 3 Tap "Install App"

Select "Install App" or "Add to Home Screen".

STEP 4 Launch Standalone

Confirm prompt. AiroDrop icon will appear on your Android Home Screen!

iPhone Setup — Step 1 of 2: WebApp (PWA)

How to Install WebApp from Safari (iOS PWA)

Step 1 of 2
STEP 1 Tap Three Dots (...)

Open AiroDrop in iPhone Safari and tap the three dots (…) button on the bottom bar.

STEP 2 Select Share

Select Share from the Safari options menu.

STEP 3 Expand Menu

Tap the down arrow (∨) or swipe up on the Share Sheet to expand options.

STEP 4 Add to Home Screen

Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen (+).

STEP 5 Tap Add to Confirm

Tap Add in the top right corner of the confirmation screen.

STEP 6 Launch AiroDrop

Open AiroDrop from your iPhone Home Screen like a native app!
